  (requested 2026-11-20 16:51).

## Text that fits by itself on the players and the clock (requested 2026-11-20 16:53)
- On the players' front (songs and radio) and on the clock front (clock, its date / weather / alarms, screen saver),
  text sizes itself automatically to what fits: long titles, station names, artist / album lines, badges and times
  shrink (down to a readable minimum) instead of being cut or wrapping badly, and use the room when there is some.
- These aren't governed by the Settings "Text size" slider (which is for Settings, wizard, help and helpers only).
- On the clock front the shrinking has a firm floor: text always stays comfortably readable (never below about 14 sp for
  the date / weather / alarms, the time digits never below a large size); when it still doesn't fit, a line is
  shortened (e.g. weekday abbreviated) rather than made smaller (requested 2026-11-20 16:54).

## More weather icons on the clock (requested 2026-11-20 16:56)
- Snow gets its own icons: snowflakes alone, and a cloud with snowflakes.
- Rain gets an icon without the cloud too: raindrops alone (the cloud with drops stays).

  moderate / heavy (rain, snow, heavy showers, freezing rain) → the symbol with its cloud.
- The weather icon shows the forecast for 12:00 noon today at the chosen city (its local time), instead of the
  weather right now; it changes to the next day's noon after midnight (requested 2026-11-20 16:58).
- Next to the weather city setting (Alarm clock → city / weather): a choice "Now / At noon" for what the clock's weather
  icon shows — the weather right now, or today's noon forecast (default: At noon) (requested 2026-11-20 17:00).
