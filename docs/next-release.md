# Next release: requests kept

## Alarm stations stay chosen (requested 2026-11-20 11:50)
- The preferred and fallback radio stations chosen last are remembered and come already selected on every new alarm.
- Editing an alarm always shows its own stations selected; an alarm without stations gets the last chosen ones.
- A station no longer in the favourites (removed or its address changed) still shows selected on the alarm, marked
  "not in favourites", instead of the choice looking empty.
